My approach

I'm an engineering student studying Electronics and Communication. I learn by building: wiring a circuit, measuring it, and finding out why it doesn't match the theory. Control and instrumentation is where that habit matters most: a controller is only as good as the measurement it acts on.

Right now I'm building my foundations in feedback systems and circuit design, and I want to apply them to real measurement and control problems. I'm also interested in trying new technologies, and I'm looking for projects and internships where I can do that.
